# REPLAY sponsorship — tiers and deliverables

Working document for REPLAY 3 (Sep 12–13, 2026, IndiQube Symphony, MG Road).
The website renders these tiers from `src/lib/sponsor-tiers.ts`. This document is
the full specification; keep the two in sync. The partner deck lives outside this
repo — if it is updated, it should say the same thing.

## Where sponsorship sits

Sponsorship is not the same product as the existing partner packages:

| Product | Price | What is being sold |
|---|---|---|
| Community engagement | ₹3,000 / ₹3,500 per day | A three-hour programme slot and four tables |
| Booth | ₹6,500 / ₹8,000 | Floor space for the weekend |
| **Sponsorship** | **₹10,000 – ₹1,00,000** | **Brand association across the whole event journey** |

A booth is a place to stand. A sponsorship is a name on the event. The site should
never let the two blur, because the ₹10,000 gaming tier sits only ₹2,000 above a
standard booth — see the note under that tier.

## The four stages

Every deliverable in every tier belongs to one of four stages. This is the frame
the partner deck uses, so the tiers slot straight into it.

| Stage | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **Before** | Website, social, email, press, the creative lockup |
| **In the room** | Banners, wristbands, welcome kits, signage, booths, the floor map |
| **In the play** | Named events in the published programme, prizes |
| **After** | Recap content, photo/video assets |

Four surfaces do the heavy lifting inside "In the room":

- **Wristband branding** — the credential every attendee wears for two straight
  days, and the single most-seen surface at the event. Carried by the title,
  association and zone tiers. See the note under [Cross-tier notes](#cross-tier-notes)
  on print area and on what this costs the title tier.
- **Banners** — large-format, hung. Two in prime positions from the ₹10,000 tier
  up; the title tier adds the main backdrop and the entrance arch.
- **Welcome kit inclusions** — near-zero marginal cost, so every tier gets an
  insert. Differentiation is in *what* goes in: title and association co-brand
  the kit itself and put a physical item in it, the rest get an insert.
- **Events in the published programme** — every tier except venue gets its name
  on something people actually sign up for. This is the deliverable that makes a
  sponsorship feel like participation rather than advertising.

The "After" stage matters more than its size suggests. Recap credit and usable
photo/video is what converts a first-time sponsor into a returning one, and it is
cheap to produce. A delivery report — real footfall, real reach — is not
currently promised at any tier; adding it would cost nothing but an afternoon and
almost nobody at this scale offers one.

## The ladder

| Tier | Ask | Slots | Credit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Title sponsor | ₹1,00,000 + GST | 1, exclusive | "X presents REPLAY" |
| In association with | ₹25,000 + GST | 1, exclusive | "REPLAY in association with X" |

| Gaming partner | ₹10,000 + GST, both days | several | Official partner venue |
| Venue partner | In kind | 1 | "Hosted at X" |

The venue figure is deliberately not published. It is carried as "in kind" on the site.

### 1. Title sponsor — ₹1,00,000

One brand, exclusive. Ten complimentary two-day passes.

- **Before**
  1. The "presents REPLAY" lockup on every event creative, the website header and the ticket page
  2. A dedicated announcement post across REPLAY channels, plus one co-created content piece
  3. Named in attendee emails from registration through the know-before-you-go
- **In the room**
  1. Wristband branding — your mark on the credential every attendee wears for two days
  2. The main backdrop and the entrance arch, plus two large-format banners in prime positions
  3. The welcome kit co-branded with the lockup, one branded item of yours in every kit, and an insert
  4. Four standees placed across the floor and a premium booth footprint in a prime floor position
  5. Named zone on the printed floor map
- **In the play**
  1. One named signature event in the published programme — "The X Cup" or equivalent
  2. Headline prize credit with the winner announcement
- **After**
  1. Named in the post-event recap, with branded photo and video assets you can use
  2. First right of refusal on the same tier for the next edition

## Cross-tier notes

**Wristband branding now runs across three tiers** — title, association and zone.
It was previously a title exclusive on the argument that the credential every
attendee wears for two days is the single most-seen surface at the event and is
worth more as one brand's. Two practical consequences to plan for: a wristband
has limited print area, so three marks plus REPLAY's own needs to be checked
against the actual artwork before it is sold; and it stops being a reason to
choose the title tier.

**"One named signature event"** now appears in four of the five tiers. Four
simultaneous signature events is not a signature — consider naming them by tier
in the programme ("presented by" for title, "in association with" for the
association tier, zone-named for zone partners) so the schedule reads as a
hierarchy rather than four equal headliners.

## In-kind and barter

Not every sponsor should pay cash. Worth keeping open, priced against the ladder:

- **Media partner** — coverage, photography or video, credited under its own
  "media partner" line rather than the association credit, which is now a single
  exclusive slot and should not be bartered away. Route existing content-creator
  enquiries here.
- **Prize partner** — games, vouchers or hardware as tournament prizes, credited
  on the prize table and in the winner announcements.
- **F&B partner** — catering or sampling rights in the gaming lounge, plus a
  welcome kit inclusion.

Price the in-kind contribution at its retail value and place it on whichever rung
that value reaches, minus the deliverables that only cash can fund.

## How this document maps to the website

This document is the full specification, grouped by the four stages. The website
states the same deliverables more compactly, because five tiers listing the same
five lines each read as repetition rather than as a ladder:

- `SPONSOR_BASELINE` in `src/lib/sponsor-tiers.ts` holds what every tier gets —
  the announcement post and co-created piece, the website logo, two banners, a
  welcome kit insert, and the recap credit. Stated once, above the cards.
- Each tier's `adds` array holds only what it carries on top.

Nothing is dropped between the two views; the site simply factors out the common
lines. If a deliverable moves in or out of the baseline here, it has to move in
`sponsor-tiers.ts` too — and the tier that previously carried it explicitly needs
that line removed from, or added to, its `adds`.

## Changing a tier

Adding, repricing or resequencing a tier is a single edit to the `SPONSOR_TIERS`
array — order in the array is order on the page. Update this document in the same
change.
